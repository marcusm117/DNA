Each clause is an English sentence representing a mathematical statement quantified by its upper-level quantifiers. The relationship between premises and conclusions is implication. The relationship between each clause is conjunction. So for clauses quantified by the same top-level universal quantifier, you can easily formalize them into the binder form.

You should try to make each clause and sub-structures as atomic as possible i.e. within a clause or a clause in a sub-structure, there shouldn't be any logical operators. 3. Formalized Structure: The structure is exactly the same as the Semi-Formalized Structure, but you will formalize each English clauses in premises and conclusions into Lean expressions, to get the Formalized Structure.
